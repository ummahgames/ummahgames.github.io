# Games hidden from the main landing page

These 41 games are temporarily hidden pending testing. Their source files, artwork, and direct game URLs are preserved. They do not appear in the main landing page, search results, filters, or Surprise me selections.

`hidden-games.json` is the visibility list used by the landing-page build. To restore a game, remove its slug from that file and run `node game-kit/build.cjs` from the workspace root. For Little Delivery Wagon or Sleepy Creature Hotel, also restore its card from Git history, since their cards are maintained by hand. Update this list after restoring games.

Spoken-name matches: “Banner Field” is **Bannerfield**, “Companions of the Road” is **Companions of the Grove**, and “Bridge Rites” is **Bridgewrights**.
